αʹWhat we do

Data engineering, analytics and automation.

Start with one and add the next when you need it. You can hire us for a single dashboard or for all three.

Data engineering

All your data in one place.

We connect your systems, clean the data and keep it in one warehouse that every report and dashboard reads, refreshed on a schedule.

What you get

  • Pipelines from each of your systems
  • Data cleaned and checked on arrival
  • One warehouse for every report
  • Systems connected to each other

Analytics

Clear dashboards and analysis.

We turn your data into dashboards and analysis that show how the business is doing and what is behind each figure.

What you get

  • Dashboards for each team
  • KPIs with one agreed definition
  • Analysis on the questions you ask
  • Views your team can filter themselves

Automation

Repeated work, automated.

We automate the tasks you repeat: generating reports, sending invoices and emails, running the same analysis each week.

What you get

  • Reports generated and sent for you
  • Invoices sent on schedule
  • Alerts when a number moves
  • AI on the steps where it saves time

βʹHow we work

How a project runs, from α to ω.

We work in short rounds and show you something working after each one. Everything we build stays yours.

  1. α

    Listen

    You walk us through the reports you rely on and the questions they can’t answer.

  2. 2

    Map

    We go through your systems and spreadsheets and tell you what the data can support today.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build in short rounds, each ending with something you can open and check.

  4. ω

    Automate

    Whatever still gets done by hand each week goes on a schedule, with an alert when it fails.
